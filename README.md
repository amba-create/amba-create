# Hello, I'm Amba!

I'm a Mathematics and Actuarial Science graduate from the University of Leicester (2:1), building toward quantitative finance and data science roles. 

## About Me

My degree combined rigorous statistical methodology (stochastic processes, Monte Carlo methods, GLMs, Bayesian inference, time series) with a lot of applied coding in R and Python. My dissertation used a 10,000-iteration Poisson-lognormal Monte Carlo simulation to compare flood insurance protection gaps in Bangladesh and Japan, and it's the project that convinced me I want to keep building models like this. I also spent time in Behavioural Science at Swiss Re, analysing customer journey data and presenting recommendations that were adopted.

I'm currently looking for graduate roles in quantitative analysis, risk modelling, or data science.

## Projects

### Quantitative Finance
- **[Stock Price Modelling with GBM](https://github.com/amba-create/gbm-stock-modelling)** — Geometric Brownian Motion applied to Tesla's share price: parameter estimation (MoM and MLE), statistical validation of the normality assumption (rejected, fat tails confirmed), and Monte Carlo forecasting with confidence bands
- **[Pairs Trading: A Cointegration Approach](https://github.com/amba-create/pairs-trading-cl-pg)** — Tested 9 candidate equity pairs for cointegration (not just correlation) using the Engle-Granger test; identified CL/PG as the only statistically significant pair (p = 0.0136) after 8 sector-obvious candidates failed
- **[Portfolio Optimisation](https://github.com/amba-create/portfolio-optimisation)** — Mean-variance optimisation and efficient frontier construction across 6 equities, with a 10,000-path Monte Carlo stress test of portfolio outcomes

### Data Science / Machine Learning
- **[Credit Risk Scoring](https://github.com/amba-create/credit-risk-scoring)** — Logistic regression, random forest, and gradient boosting classifiers to predict loan defaults, with explicit analysis of the class imbalance problem and its implications for model choice
- **[Bayesian Inference and Regression Modelling](https://github.com/amba-create/bayesian-inference-regression-modelling)** — Beta-Binomial Bayesian updating for an investment decision, plus a forward-selection linear model and a Gamma GLM compared head-to-head on the same salary data
- **[Time Series Forecasting with SARIMA](https://github.com/amba-create/time-series-sarima-forecasting)** — Box-Jenkins SARIMA models for Rome's monthly temperatures, validated out-of-sample against real 2023-2024 observations rather than stopping at an in-sample fit
- **[Education Participation: Data Cleaning and Regional Analysis](https://github.com/amba-create/education-participation-data-cleaning)** — Tidyverse data cleaning on a messy UK government dataset, validated by reconstructing national totals from local-authority data and checking they match exactly
- **[Behavioural Finance: Prospect Theory and Market Anomalies](https://github.com/amba-create/behavioural-finance-prospect-theory)** — Diagnosing and correcting a mis-specified Prospect Theory value function, applying it to a real lottery choice, and reviewing the evidence on Bayesian biases in financial decision-making

### Risk Modelling
- **[Flood Protection Gap Simulation](https://github.com/amba-create/flood-protection-gap-dissertation)** — Dissertation project: Poisson-lognormal Monte Carlo model comparing flood insurance protection gaps in Bangladesh and Japan, including an honest accounting of the model's calibration limitations
- **[Insurance Claims Reserving: Chain Ladder Analysis](https://github.com/amba-create/claims-reserving-chain-ladder)** — Chain Ladder reserving on a real insurer's published claims triangle, benchmarked against their actual published reserves
- **[Climate Variables and Insurance Loss Ratios](https://github.com/amba-create/climate-insurance-loss-glm)** — Group project: Gamma GLM testing whether cyclone frequency, flood frequency, rainfall, and sea level explain insurance loss ratios over time
- **[Estimating an Inheritance with a Markov + Monte Carlo Model](https://github.com/amba-create/markov-inheritance-model)** — Gompertz mortality modelling combined with a stochastic stock-price model to estimate the distribution of an inherited sum, validated by matching Monte Carlo output against closed-form results
- **[Pan-Trap Insect Analysis](https://github.com/amba-create/pan-trap-insect-analysis)** — Group project: five years of ecological survey data cleaned and analysed for species trends and community composition
